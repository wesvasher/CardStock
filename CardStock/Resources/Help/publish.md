---
title: Publish your site
category: Publishing
summary: Send your changes to GitHub and your live site.
order: 2
---

1. Click **Publish** in the toolbar, or the big button on the [Publishing](cardstock:publishing) page.
2. Optionally describe your changes, such as "New post about Japan".
3. Click **Publish**.

CardStock generates the flat HTML website, saves a snapshot of your changes, picks up anything you published from another Mac, and sends it all to GitHub. Your host then updates the live site, usually within a minute.

## Publish later

1. On the [Publishing](cardstock:publishing) page, click **Schedule Publish…** under the Publish button.
2. Pick a quick choice such as **Tomorrow, 9 AM**, or choose a date on the calendar and set the time.
3. Optionally describe your changes, then click **Schedule**.

A countdown shows how long is left. Click **Change…** to pick another time, or **Remove** to cancel it.

At that time CardStock publishes everything on this Mac, just as if you'd clicked Publish. Posts marked **Draft** stay hidden, so turn Draft off before the scheduled time for anything that should go live.

> Keep CardStock open with the site. If your Mac is asleep or CardStock is closed at that time, it publishes as soon as they're back. If publishing fails, for example with no internet, the countdown turns red with a **Try Again** button.

## Is everything published?

The Publishing page shows:

- **Unpublished changes** on this Mac
- **Newer updates** published from another Mac, in a purple banner that says what changed and what to do
- When you last published

The sidebar shows how many changes are waiting next to **Publishing**. A purple badge there means another Mac has published updates this Mac doesn't have yet. CardStock checks for them when you switch to it and every few minutes.

## See the live site

Click **Open Website** in the toolbar. It opens the web address from [Site Details](cardstock:site-details) in your browser.

> Something missing? Check it isn't still a **Draft**.
