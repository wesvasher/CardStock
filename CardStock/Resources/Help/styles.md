A Template is a saved set of look choices: fonts, colors, corners and width.

## Save a Template

1. Set things up the way you like in [Configuration](cardstock:configuration).
2. Next to "not saved", click **Save as New Template…** and give it a name.

## Switch Templates

Choose a Template from the **Template** menu in [Templates](cardstock:templates) in Configuration. Your current, unsaved look stays in the menu until you quit, so you can switch back.

**CardStock Default** is always available.

## Manage Templates

Use the **Manage** menu to:

- **Update** a Template with the site's current look
- **Rename**, **Export** or **Delete** it

## Share a Template

**Export…** saves a `.cardstockstyle` file. Send it to someone, and they can double-click it or use **Import…** to add it to their CardStock.

Templates are kept on your Mac and work with every site. Using one copies its settings into the site, so the site doesn't depend on it.
