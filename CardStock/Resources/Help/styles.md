A Template is a set of look choices: fonts, colors, width, corners, and how buttons and cards look.

## Pick a Template

Open [Templates](cardstock:templates) in Configuration and click a card. Each card is a small preview drawn in the template's own colors and styles. The fonts are shown under it, headings first. A dark strip down the right side means the template has a dark version for visitors whose device is set to dark mode.

CardStock comes with seven:

| Template | Fonts | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| CardStock Default | Serif text, sans serif headings | The starter look |
| Journal | Lora, Playfair Display (serif) | Warm and bookish, for writing |
| Studio | Inter, Space Grotesk (sans serif) | Bold and roomy, for portfolios |
| Editorial | Source Serif, Libre Baskerville (serif) | Classic newspaper, square edges |
| Night | DM Sans (sans serif) | Dark, modern |
| Minimal | IBM Plex Sans (sans serif) | Black and white, lots of space |
| Garden | Work Sans with Fraunces headings | Soft, rounded and friendly |

After picking one, change anything on the Fonts, Colors, Layout and Components pages. Your look before switching stays as **Unsaved Template** until you quit, so you can switch back.

## Save a Template

1. Set things up the way you like in [Configuration](cardstock:configuration).
2. In [Templates](cardstock:templates), click **Save Current Look…** under My Templates and give it a name.

## Manage Templates

Right-click a saved template, or click its **⋯** button, to:

- **Use** it for this site
- **Update** it with the site's current look
- **Rename**, **Export** or **Delete** it

## Share a Template

**Export…** saves a `.cardstockstyle` file. Send it to someone, and they can double-click it or use **Import…** to add it to their CardStock.

Templates are kept on your Mac and work with every site. Using one copies its settings into the site, so the site doesn't depend on it.
