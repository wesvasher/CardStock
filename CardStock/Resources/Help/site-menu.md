Click **Configuration** in the sidebar, then [Site Details](cardstock:site-details) at the top of the list.

## Site

- **Title** appears at the top of every page and in browser tabs.
- **Description** is a sentence about the site, used by search engines and RSS readers.
- **Author** is your name.
- **Web address** is where your site lives online, such as `https://my-site.pages.dev`. The toolbar's **Open Website** button opens it.
- **Language** is a code such as `en` or `fr`.

## Site Icon

The icon people see when they add your site to the home screen of an iPhone, iPad, or Android phone or tablet. It's also the small icon in browser tabs (the favicon).

1. Next to **Image**, click **Choose…** and pick a square image from your site's Files, or click **Choose from Mac…** to copy one in. 1024 × 1024 pixels is ideal; PNG and JPEG work best.
2. **Preview** shows it the way an iPhone (rounded corners), Android (a circle) and a browser tab show it.
3. **Background** fills any transparent parts of the image on iPhone and iPad, and the space around it on Android. Pick a color that matches your icon.
4. **Name on home screen** is the label under the icon. Keep it short. Leave it empty to use the title.

When you publish, CardStock makes every size those devices need.

**Use a different favicon** is off unless you turn it on. Off, browser tabs show a small version of the site icon. On, choose any image for **Favicon**, such as a GIF or an `.ico` file, and it's used just as it is.

The top bar's logo and its left, center and right links are in [Top Navigation Bar](cardstock:top-bar), and the footer has its own page, [Footer](cardstock:footer). See [Change fonts, colors and layout](help:theming).
