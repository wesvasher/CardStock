When you have lots of pages, put them in folders in the [Pages](cardstock:pages) list. Folders only organize the list in CardStock. Pages keep their web addresses, and visitors never see the folders.

## Make a folder

Click the folder button next to **New Page…** at the bottom of the list. To make a folder inside another one, right-click that folder and choose **New Folder Inside…**.

## Open and close folders

Click the triangle next to a folder to show its pages, and click it again to hide them. You can also double-click the folder.

## Put pages in folders

- **Drag** a page onto a folder. If you hold it over a closed folder for a moment, the folder opens so you can drop it into a folder inside.
- **Right-click** a page and choose **Move To**.
- To take a page out of its folders, drag it to the empty space below the list, or choose **Move To › Pages (top level)**.

To move several pages at once, select them first: ⌘-click to add pages one at a time, or ⇧-click to select a run of pages. Then drag any one of them, or right-click and choose **Move Pages To**. The editor shows the page you clicked last.

Folders can be dragged into other folders too.

## Rename or remove a folder

Right-click the folder. **Remove Folder…** only removes the folder: its pages move up a level, and no pages are deleted.

## Searching and filtering

When you search or choose a filter, the list shows every matching page from all folders, and each page says which folder it's in.

## How page folders differ from Files folders

| | Pages list folders | Files page folders |
|---|---|---|
| What they are | Labels that organize the list in CardStock | Real folders inside your site's `media` folder |
| Seen in Finder | No: every page stays in `pages/` | Yes |
| Change web addresses | Never | Yes: `/media/photos/beach.jpg` |
| Names | Anything, with capitals and spaces | Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes |
| Moving something | Only changes where it shows in the list | Moves the file and updates every link to it |

Both kinds travel with your site, so they look the same on your other Macs after you publish. Learn more about [folders on the Files page](help:files).

> These folders are also different from [short addresses](help:folders) in Configuration → Folders, which change where a page can be found on your site.
