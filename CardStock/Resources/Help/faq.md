### Why isn't my post on the live site?

- It may still be a **Draft**. Turn Draft off above the text.
- You may not have published since. Click **Publish**.
- Your host can take a minute or two to update after publishing.

### The preview and the live site look different.

The preview shows your latest changes, including drafts. The live site shows what you last published. Publish to bring them in line.

### Do I need to save?

No. CardStock saves as you type. ⌘S saves right away if you like.

### Where are my files?

In your site folder. Choose **Site → Show in Finder**. See [What's in a site folder](help:site-folder).

### My image doesn't show up.

Check that the image's line hasn't been changed. To add it again, find it on the [Files](cardstock:files) page and click **Copy Markdown**, then paste it into the page.

### Can I edit the files in another app?

Yes, they're plain text. Choose **Site → Reload from Disk** (⇧⌘R) afterwards if CardStock was open.

### How much space am I using on GitHub?

Open [Site Details](cardstock:site-details) in Configuration and look at **Storage**. It shows the size GitHub reports for your repository, compared with GitHub's suggestion of staying under about 1 GB, and warns about any file too big for GitHub (100 MB) or Cloudflare (25 MB). Deleting files doesn't make the repository smaller, because GitHub keeps every published version, so optimized images are the best way to stay small.

### Is my GitHub token safe?

It's kept in your Mac's Keychain and is never saved in the site folder or uploaded. To remove it, open **CardStock → Settings…** and click **Sign Out…** next to the account.

### How do I update CardStock?

Open **CardStock → Settings…** (⌘,) and click **Check for a New Version**. CardStock doesn't update itself; the Settings window has the steps.

### Can I make CardStock itself dark?

Yes. In **CardStock → Settings…**, choose **Auto**, **Light** or **Dark**. Your website's colors are set separately in [Colors](cardstock:colors) in Configuration.
