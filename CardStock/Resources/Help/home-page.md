The Home page is the first page in [Pages](cardstock:pages). You can also open it from the **Edit Home Page** tile on the Start screen.

1. Click **Pages** in the sidebar, then click **Home** in the list of pages. It opens in the editor, with the preview on the right.
2. Write an introduction in Markdown. Images work here too.
3. Your recent posts are listed below the introduction automatically.

The Home page is the file `pages/index.md`, published at your site's main address.

> If the Home page's text doesn't appear in the preview, your site was made with an older version of CardStock. Open [Configuration](cardstock:configuration) and click **Update Theme Files**.
