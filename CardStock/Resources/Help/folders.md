Every address on your site is made of folders: `/blog/`, `/pages/`, `/shop/pages/blue-mug/`. See them all in [Folders](cardstock:folders) in Configuration.

## Folders CardStock makes

Some folders are made and used by CardStock, and are marked **Made by CardStock** with a lock:

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| One per collection, like `/blog/` or `/shop/` | The collection's main page, and its items and categories in `/shop/pages/` |
| `/pages/` | Every page that isn't in a collection, like About and Search |
| `/tags/` | A page for each tag |
| `/media/` | Your photos and files |
| `/assets/` | Your theme's styles, fonts and scripts |

They can't be deleted or used for anything else. Making a collection adds its folder; deleting the collection removes it.

## Make a short address

A folder you make is a short, easy address for one of your pages. For a mailing list page at `/pages/subscribe/`, you might make `/subscribe/`, so visitors can type `www.yoursite.com/subscribe/`.

1. In [Folders](cardstock:folders), click **New Folder…**.
2. Type the folder's name, like `subscribe`. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
3. Choose the page it **Shows**: any page, a collection's main page, an item or a category.
4. Click **Create**, then publish.

The page shows at both addresses. Its own address doesn't change, and search engines are told that one is the original, so the page isn't counted twice. Click the eye button to preview a folder, or change the page it shows from its menu.

To link to the short address, choose it from the page menu next to a link in [Top Navigation Bar](cardstock:top-bar) or [Footer](cardstock:footer), under **Your Folders**. In [Pages](cardstock:pages), a page with a short address shows "Also /subscribe/".

## Delete a short address

Click the trash button next to the folder and confirm. Only the short address goes away; the page stays at its own address. Links to the short address stop working, so update any that use it.

If a folder's page is deleted or turned into a draft, the folder isn't published until you choose another page or publish the page again. The Start screen's health check lists folders whose page is gone.
